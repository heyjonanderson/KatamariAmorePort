# Assumptions and placeholders

Living list. Every guess, stand-in value, or shortcut gets an entry so it can be cleaned up later.

Status:
- VERIFIED: checked against the data in a way that could have failed.
- GUESS: believed, not checked. Needs a test or a look at the engine code.
- PLACEHOLDER: a temporary choice made to keep moving. Replace with the engine's value.
- LIMIT: a limit of the tools or process, not of the data.

Last updated: 2026-11-03, after the first playable (game.html).
